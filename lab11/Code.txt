Lab 11 - Column Calculations and Measures - DAX for copy and paste
Copy each formula from here rather than the lab book. Three lab book typos are fixed here:
[MiddleName] has no space, the quotes are straight quotes, and "3-Low" has no trailing space.

Exercise 1 - Customer table, New column
FullName = [FirstName] & " " & IF(ISBLANK([MiddleName]),"",[MiddleName] & " ") & [LastName] & IF(ISBLANK([Title]),""," (" & [Title] & ")")

Exercise 2 - Web Sales table, then Reseller Sales table, New column
CostAmount = RELATED('Product'[StandardCost]) * [OrderQuantity]

Exercise 3 - Reseller table, New column (twice)
Reseller Sales = SUMX(RELATEDTABLE('Reseller Sales'),[SalesAmount])
Reseller Group = SWITCH(TRUE(),ISBLANK([Reseller Sales]),"4-No Sales", [Reseller Sales]>400000,"1-Premium", [Reseller Sales]>100000,"2-High","3-Low")

Exercise 4 - Web Sales table, New measure (each one)
Web Sales Amount = SUM('Web Sales'[SalesAmount])
Web Quantity = SUM('Web Sales'[OrderQuantity])
Web Cost Amount = SUM('Web Sales'[CostAmount])
Web Average Sale = DIVIDE([Web Sales Amount],[Web Quantity],0)
Web Margin = [Web Sales Amount]-[Web Cost Amount]

Exercise 5 - Web Sales table, New measure (each one)
Web Sales YTD = IF(ISBLANK([Web Sales Amount]),BLANK(), TOTALYTD([Web Sales Amount],'Order Date'[RealDate]))

Web Sales Amount SPLY = IF(ISBLANK([Web Sales Amount]),BLANK(),
CALCULATE([Web Sales Amount],SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR
('Order Date'[RealDate])))

Web Sales Change YonY = IF(ISBLANK([Web Sales Amount]),BLANK(),[Web Sales Amount] - [Web Sales Amount SPLY])

Web Sales Change MonM = IF(ISBLANK([Web Sales Amount]),BLANK(),[Web Sales Amount] - CALCULATE([Web Sales Amount],PARALLELPERIOD
('Order Date'[RealDate],-1,MONTH)))

Web Sales %Change YonY = IF(ISBLANK([Web Sales Amount]) || ISBLANK([Web Sales Amount SPLY]),
BLANK(),DIVIDE([Web Sales Amount],[Web Sales Amount SPLY],BLANK())-1)
